PHVD — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026 · Developer: Khorshid Mohammad

What this app collects

Nothing. PHVD does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data.

  • Measurements you enter are held on your device only.
  • There are no accounts and no login.
  • There is no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.
  • No data is shared with the developer or any third party.

Data you enter

Any clinical measurements you enter are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded. They are stored locally on the device so your series persists between sessions, and are removed when you clear the history or delete the app.

Intended use

PHVD is an educational and reference tool for qualified healthcare professionals. It is not a medical device, does not provide a diagnosis, and does not replace clinical assessment or local protocols. It is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app sends any data off the device, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version ships.

Use the app in your browser

The same tool runs as a web app, with no install and no account: open the PHVD web app. It works offline once loaded, and everything you enter stays on your own device.
