# Privacy Policy — PHVD

**Last updated:** 13 July 2026
**Developer:** Khorshid Mohammad

Apple requires a publicly reachable privacy policy URL before an app can be reviewed.
Host this page (GitHub Pages on the PHVD repo is free and sufficient) and paste the URL
into App Store Connect > App Information > Privacy Policy URL.

## What this app collects

**Nothing.** PHVD does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data.

- Measurements you enter are held in memory on your device for the duration of the session.
- There are no accounts, no login, and no analytics SDK.
- There is no advertising and no third-party tracking.
- No data is shared with the developer or any third party.

## Data you enter

Any clinical measurements you enter are processed entirely on your device. They are not
uploaded. If you close the app, they are discarded unless you have explicitly saved them
to your device.

> **If the AI report feature is included in the shipped build, this section must change.**
> Sending clinical text to the Gemini API means data leaves the device and this policy —
> and the App Privacy questionnaire — must say so. See ANDROID/IOS notes in README.

## Children

PHVD is intended for qualified healthcare professionals. It is not directed at children.
